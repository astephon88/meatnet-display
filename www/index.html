Probes

Tick Relay to hold a connection to a probe: the app then gets its full status (predictions, alarms, history) and can send it commands through this device. Every probe heard is also re-advertised (live temperatures only).
ProbeCore / Surf / AmbSourceRelay

MEATER bridge

Tick a MEATER to connect to it and publish it as a Combustion probe (ambient → T8 ambient, the 5 tips spread across T1–T7, core = lowest of T1–T6). Each bridged MEATER uses a relay slot, and the MEATER app can't connect to it at the same time. While bridging, scanning is active so MEATERs can be found.
